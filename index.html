BEGINNER SPELL No. 007

Friendship Jar Spell

a simple charm for calling warm & honest friendship into your life

☾ ✦ ☽ MAIN RITUAL IMAGE images/ritual-hero.jpg recommended 900 × 520 px

01. WHAT YOU WILL NEED

0/5 materials gathered

02. INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Place the token inside the jar.

    Think of the kind of friendship you want to welcome.

  2. Pour honey over the token.

    Honey invites sweetness, warmth, and affection.

  3. Add one small flower.

    Choose one that reminds you of a new beginning.

  4. Seal the jar.

    Hold it in both hands and focus on your intention.

  5. Light a tea light above the jar.

    Close your eyes. When you are ready, speak your wish aloud.

WORDS TO SPEAK

“May friendship find me,
may kindness stay,
may new connections
come my way.”
✦ ✦ ✦
PLEASE REMEMBER: This charm is meant to invite connection, not control a specific person. Never leave a burning candle unattended.
